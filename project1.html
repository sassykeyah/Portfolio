An interactive narrative built in 24 hours that reframes 404 errors as a metaphor for digital disconnection. Following the journey from lost (404) to found (302).

Chapter 01

The challenge

The University of Canberra's 24-hour Design Challenge set the theme "Lost and Found" and asked entrants to ship a digital design — concept, design, code, and deployment — within a single day.

Twenty-four hours to take an idea from blank page to deployed site is barely enough time to second-guess yourself, let alone iterate. The constraints I had to design within:

  • Devising a narrative that used the theme effectively, requiring fast storyboarding and ideation.
  • Integrating complex scroll interactions within tight time constraints.
  • Creating a seamless user experience that conveyed the 404-error metaphor without explanation.
Chapter 02

My approach

The solution focused on building an immersive scroll-driven experience that guides a user from 404 (lost) to 302 (found) through interactive storytelling. Three pillars carried the work:

Concept

  • Explored digital disconnection as the emotional core.
  • Reframed 404 errors as a metaphor for being "lost" online.
  • Designed the journey toward "found" as the user's reward, not a given.

Design

  • CMD-console aesthetic for familiarity and nostalgia.
  • Monospaced type with a high-contrast palette to evoke the terminal.
  • Interaction constraints that enforce the narrative pace.

Build

  • GSAP-driven scroll triggers reveal the story progressively.
  • Click interactions act as deliberate "next-step" prompts.
  • Deployed to GitHub Pages within the 24-hour window.
404 → 302

Lost, then found

Chapter 03

What I built

  • Narrative-controlled progression - users can only advance by clicking or answering intended prompts, mirroring the deliberate pace of being "lost."
  • Layered GSAP animation - split text, glitch effects, and smooth scrolling that reinforce the 'lost' theme.
  • End-to-end in 24 hours - conceptualised, designed, built, and deployed within the challenge window.
